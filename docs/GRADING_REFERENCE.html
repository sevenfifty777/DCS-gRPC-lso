LSO Grading Reference

This lightweight HTML companion was refreshed on 2026-08-25. The canonical, complete, and repository-reviewable specification is GRADING_REFERENCE.md.

LSO applies a simplified geometric training grade. It samples glideslope and lineup on the first inbound crossing of 3/4, 1/2, and 1/4 nm while the hook is below 500 ft above the deck. AoA controls chart color but does not affect the grade.

Important limits

  • The worst available gate snapshot drives the recovered grade; missing gates are skipped.
  • AoA, power, speed, sink rate, wind, weight, and deviation duration are not graded.
  • A hook-up qualification pass is displayed as Qualif Bolter; without an estimated cable it receives B.
  • Internal Unknown tracks are discarded in live mode.
